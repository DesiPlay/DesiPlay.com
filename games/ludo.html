Ludo

Roll a six, bring your tokens home and race your friends.

Game controls

🎲

Red starts. Roll a six to bring a token out.

How to play Ludo
  • Four players share one device. Each player has four tokens.
  • Roll a six to move a token out of the yard. A six gives another roll.
  • Tap one of your highlighted movable tokens after rolling.
  • Land on an opponent on a non-safe square to send that token back to its yard.
  • Star squares are safe. Your token must roll the exact number to reach home.
  • First player to bring all four tokens home wins. This is a local pass-and-play version; online multiplayer is not included.
