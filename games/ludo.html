How to play · rules
  • Players: choose 2, 3 or 4 local players. Two-player mode uses Red and Yellow; three-player mode uses Red, Blue and Yellow.
  • Leave the yard: roll a six to move a token onto its own starting square. A six gives you another roll.
  • Move: after rolling, select one of your highlighted tokens. Tokens travel around the shared 52-square track, then enter their own coloured home lane. You need the exact number to reach the centre.
  • Capture: land on a single opponent token on a non-safe square to send it back to its yard. A capture earns an extra roll.
  • Safe squares: the star squares and all four starting squares are safe. Tokens on a safe square cannot be captured.
  • Blockades: two tokens of the same colour on one track square form a blockade. No token may pass through it or land on it. Moving one of the pair breaks the blockade.
  • Three sixes: if you roll three sixes in a row, the third roll is ignored and your turn ends.
  • Extra rolls: get another roll for a six, a capture or bringing a token exactly home. The first player to bring all four tokens home wins.

This is a local pass-and-play game for one shared device. Online multiplayer, accounts and cloud saves are not included.
