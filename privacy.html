YOUR DATA, YOUR DEVICE

Privacy notice

DesiPlay's current browser games are designed to work without an account.

What is stored?

Game scores, best results and display preferences may be saved in your browser's local storage on this device. They are not synced to a server by these game pages.

Offline cache

If you install or revisit DesiPlay online, a service worker can cache the website files so supported pages can load offline. You can clear this data from your browser settings.

Third-party services

The stylesheet currently requests fonts from Google Fonts when internet access is available. The site does not currently implement sign-in, online multiplayer, analytics or advertising integrations.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact details published by the DesiPlay project owner. This notice should be reviewed and updated before adding analytics, ads, accounts or online services.
